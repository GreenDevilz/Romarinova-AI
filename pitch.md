# Pitch and Impact

## 1. Impact and future work

### 1.1 Prioritized developable compound-variety candidates

Romarinova AI delivers prioritized developable compound-variety candidates with provenance and evidence. Impact is quantified by actual eligible rosemary compound N, shortlist N, and U, with the empty/short shortlist narrative preserving honesty.

### 1.2 Modest-shortlist framing

If the shortlist is dominated by smaller phenolic and monoterpenoid constituents and the most rosemary-characteristic diterpenes (carnosic acid, carnosol) appear only in the diagnostics data, the narrative frames this as prioritization discipline: the pipeline correctly separates developable candidates from large, PAINS-prone, low-bioavailability diterpenes, and the diagnostics data preserves them for experimental follow-up. This is a reported finding, not a failure.

### 1.3 Side-by-side comparison view

The α-Glucosidase Screening page shows the locked ranking and the ligand-only ranking side by side and simultaneously. This makes the domain-shift response concrete and lets a viewer see how much of the shortlist is ML-driven versus similarity- and developability-driven.

### 1.4 Compound-level view

The compound-level view is the primary traceability artifact, with U reported, `ligand_only_score`/`ligand_only_rank` included, `primary_cultivar` (display-only) and `all_cultivars` (authoritative) both shown with a tooltip.

### 1.5 Future work

- Structural assessment (e.g., docking or MD) on a curated, unambiguous subset.
- Extension to other glycosidases (α-amylase, lipase).
- Sensitivity to alternative fingerprints (ECFP6) and tautomer standardization.
- Prospective experimental validation of the top shortlist candidates.

## 2. Pitch summary

Romarinova AI turns fragmented rosemary chemotype chemistry into a reproducible, evidence-traced prioritization system. It links documented Tunisian rosemary chemistry to machine learning, a bounded evidence record, and a three-page web interface. The contribution is variety- and chemotype-level traceability, scaffold-aware validation with honest uncertainty, developability filtering, a side-by-side ML-independent comparison view, and a reproducible open pipeline. It does not claim rosemary cures diabetes; it identifies which documented rosemary compounds merit further experimental investigation and shows the evidence behind each ranking.

Closing line: *Romarinova AI does not claim rosemary cures diabetes; it turns fragmented rosemary chemotype chemistry into a reproducible, evidence-traced prioritization system.*
